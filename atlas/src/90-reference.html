Glossary

Clinical and platform terms as this codebase uses them.

TermMeaning here
PKPharmacokinetics: how a drug's concentration in the body changes over time after doses.
TDMTherapeutic drug monitoring: measuring drug levels and adjusting doses to keep them in range.
TDMSThe original PrecisePK desktop product. Models named "TDMS" are the legacy single models carried over from it.
Prior, posteriorPrior: the population parameters a published study predicts for this patient. Posterior: the individual parameters after fitting to measured levels. In code these are pk_bayes_prior and pk_bayes.
MAP fitMaximum a posteriori estimation: the most probable parameters given both the prior and the measured levels.
Levenberg-MarquardtThe optimizer the engine uses for the MAP fit. It blends gradient descent with Gauss-Newton steps.
CV, RSDCoefficient of variation, also called relative SD: the SD divided by the mean. It is stored in fields named sd.
CL, Vd, Vdβ, VcClearance (L/h); volume of distribution (L); terminal-phase volume; central-compartment volume.
AUC, AUC24, AUICArea under the concentration-time curve; the same over 24 h; AUC divided by MIC.
MICMinimum inhibitory concentration of an antibiotic against the organism.
Trough, peakThe lowest level just before a dose; the highest level after one. The peak's timing is set by the peak-time protocol.
SCr, CrCl, eGFRSerum creatinine; creatinine clearance (estimated by a formula such as Cockcroft-Gault, or measured); estimated glomerular filtration rate.
BSA, IBW, LBM, dosing weightBody surface area; ideal body weight; lean body mass; the weight a model uses, chosen by the engine's rules.
AKI; KDIGO, RIFLE, AKINAcute kidney injury, and the three staging criteria the engine evaluates.
RRT, CRRTRenal replacement therapy (dialysis), and its continuous form.
NomogramA table of regimen options, here 3 intervals by 3 infusion times.
Protocol settingsInstitution rules for maximum dose, peak time, default target inputs and infusion time, matched to patients by criteria.
EHR, FHIR R4Electronic health record; HL7's REST standard for clinical data, version R4.
SMART on FHIRThe OAuth-based standard for launching an app from inside an EHR with patient context.
MAR, CSNMedication administration record; Epic's contact serial number, which identifies the encounter.
MRN, EPIMedical record number; Epic's enterprise patient identifier.
LOINC, RxNormCode systems for lab observations and for medications.
Sentri7An external clinical platform that pushes patient data into PrecisePK.
License, institution, siteA license belongs to an institution, which has its own MySQL schema. Multi-site institutions divide users and cases by site, using Cognito groups.
User pool, identity poolCognito user directory (six of them here); Cognito's exchange of a login for temporary AWS credentials, used by Main's user administration.
Blue-green laneThe v1 or v2 path segment that routes a frontend to one of two backend deployments (RX-3666).
ValkeyThe Redis-compatible cache used in AWS.
N-API, prebuildNode's stable native-addon interface; a native binary compiled ahead of time for one platform.
RX-####, BLITZ-…Jira keys. RX is the main project. BLITZ keys come from release-hotfix work.
PHI, BAAProtected health information under HIPAA; a business associate agreement with a vendor that handles PHI.
